The Problem

Q-Commerce is a
margin trap for most brands.

Brands enter quick commerce chasing growth. What they don't see until it's too late are the layers of fees that silently consume their margins — commission, fulfillment, mandatory ad spend and listing costs stacked on top of each other.

📦

Platform Commission

Blinkit charges 18%, Zepto 20%, Instamart 15% — on top of every rupee of revenue. Before you have shipped a single order.

📢

Mandatory Ad Spend

Zepto demands Rs 5,00,000 per month minimum ad spend. Blinkit Rs 2,50,000. Without it, your products are invisible on the platform.

🚚

Fulfilment and Listing Fees

Rs 40-50 per order in fulfilment fees. Rs 25,000 per SKU listing on Blinkit. These stack on top of commission and ad spend.
